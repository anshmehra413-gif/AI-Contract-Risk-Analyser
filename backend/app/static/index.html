Supervised Datasets (Section 5)

Primary Dataset

CUAD (Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset)

510 commercial contracts with 13,000+ expert annotations across 41 categories. System targets the core 15 clause categories (Termination, Liability, Indemnification, Governing Law, etc.).

Secondary Dataset

ContractNLI

607 NDA-focused contracts with entailment/contradiction hypothesis annotations used for evidence retrieval and reasoning inspiration.

Mentor Review Note: The project positioning is strictly AI-assisted legal contract analysis. It does not replace a qualified lawyer or make autonomous legal judgments.
